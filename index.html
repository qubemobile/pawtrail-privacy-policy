PawTrail Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

PawTrail ("the game") is made by Qube Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • PawTrail has no accounts or sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email address or contacts.
  • Your progress, settings and hints are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following on your device so it can work offline:

  • your level progress and achievements;
  • your hint balance and daily gift status;
  • your sound, music and vibration settings;
  • an ad-timing counter, so that ads are spaced out fairly.

We cannot see this information. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

PawTrail shows full-screen ads between levels and optional video ads that you can watch for a free hint. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, personalise (where you allow it), limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent the first time you open it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

Children

PawTrail is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Permissions

The game uses internet and network-state access to load ads, and vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. Some advertising-related permissions are added by Google's Mobile Ads SDK.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
